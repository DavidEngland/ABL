# ABL Project Glossary — concise definitions

- Obukhov length (L)  
  Definition: L = −u_*^3 θ / (κ g <w'θ'>). Scale where shear and buoyancy balance. Units: m.  
  Note (sign): L can be positive (stable regime) or negative (unstable regime). All ζ-based diagnostics use
  ζ = z / L; therefore ζ inherits the sign of L. When L<0 (convective/unstable), ζ is negative and the appropriate
  branch of φ_m/φ_h (unstable forms) must be used. When L>0 (stable) use the stable-branch forms. Do not replace ζ by |ζ|
  without explicit justification — branch behavior matters for series expansions and curvature signs.

- Dimensionless height (ζ)  
  Definition: ζ = z / L. Positive (ζ>0) indicates stable conditions; negative (ζ<0) indicates unstable conditions.
