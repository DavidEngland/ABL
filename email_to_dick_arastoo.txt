Subject: Interactive MOST curvature visualization for SBL teaching & outreach

Hi Dick and Arastoo,

I put together an interactive MOST curvature visualization to help students see how Δ and grid spacing drive stability bias: https://claude.ai/public/artifacts/6887cbe9-1440-40aa-b296-4062bc18af14

Highlights
- Curvature regimes: Δ < 0 concave-down bias, Δ = 0 neutral, Δ > 0 rare.
- Grid correction: master equation G(ζ, Δz) with live slider for Δz.
- Bias impacts: shows B = Ri_g(z_g)/Ri_b shrinking from ~2.0 to ~1.3 with the fix.
- Custom parameters: students can vary a_m, a_h and see Δ update in real time.

Why it matters
- Makes the “first 100 m” message tangible for atmospheric science students.
- Good companion for our SBL lectures, WRF/CMAQ training, and outreach.
