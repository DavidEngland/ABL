Met Tower (60 m):
  z=2m:   Sonic anemometer (u,v,w), T_sonic, RH
  z=6m:   Cup anemometer (U), T, q
  z=10m:  Cup anemometer (U), T, q (WMO standard)
  z=20m:  Cup anemometer (U only)
  z=40m:  Cup anemometer (U only)

Eddy Covariance System (10 m):
  - Open-path IRGA (CO₂, H₂O) → w'q', w'T'
  - Sonic anemometer → u', v', w', T'_sonic
  - 10 Hz sampling → 30-min covariances

Radiometer (2 m):
  - Net radiometer (SW↓, SW↑, LW↓, LW↑)
  - Pyranometer (PAR)

Surface sensors:
  - Ground-level T thermistor (2 cm depth)
